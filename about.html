About This Project

I’m Tia Shinholster, and this site is a personal archive built from twenty objects that carried me through my second year of college. These pieces may look small or ordinary at first, but each one represents a moment, a challenge, or a source of comfort that helped shape who I became during that year. Some items reminded me to slow down, some pushed me forward, and others simply grounded me when everything felt chaotic.

This archive is more than a collection—it’s a reflection of the routines, memories, and emotional anchors that supported my well-being. Each object holds a story: why I kept it, the role it played in my day-to-day life, and what it reveals about navigating growth, stress, and self-discovery. You’ll find those stories on each item’s dedicated page, told with honesty and intention.

My hope is that by sharing these pieces of my journey, others might recognize their own quiet sources of strength—the small things that matter more than we often realize. This project is both a record of the past and a reminder to honor the objects and rituals that help us move forward.
